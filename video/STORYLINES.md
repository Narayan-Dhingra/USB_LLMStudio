# Storylines — USB LLM Studio promo (~40 s, 1920×1080, narrated)

## A — "The USB drive" (RECOMMENDED)

Device: one object — the USB drive itself — carries the whole film. Form: object-to-object chain; the plug becomes the app, each feature card hands off to the next.

Viewer sees a drive plug in, a terminal spring to life, then the real product screens swap in one after another while the voice says what each does. Feels like a magic trick that is also literally true.

First 2 s: extreme close on a USB connector sliding into a port; the orange brand tile is the drive's LED — it lights on contact.

Beats: plug → terminal self-setup → chat → images → speech in/out → model manager → end card.

Look: near-black ground (#0a0a0b), Inter tight, the brand orange (#ff6a3d) used only for the LED, callouts and the end mark.

Sound: near-silent until the plug lands (soft click + LED ping), light pulse under the screens, riser into the mark.

## B — "Offline"

Device: a wifi-off toggle flipped, then the studio keeps working anyway. Form: cut on the beat — giant kinetic type fields (NO CLOUD / NO ACCOUNT / NO TRACKING) alternating with real screens.

Punchier and louder; weaker hook (starts on an icon, not the product) and the type rows do more telling than showing.

## C — "Four doors"

Device: a stylized desk with four stations — chat, images, transcribe, voice — the camera travels left to right as a flight through one world.

Prettiest world, but the desk metaphor costs build time for no extra meaning, and the screens read smaller in a wide flight.
